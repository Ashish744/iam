We started with one simple frustration

Too many passwords, too many forgotten accounts and too little visibility. We built Stackly so identity could be the strongest part of security instead of the weakest.

  • Every identity verified, every request explained
  • Security that people actually enjoy using
  • Evidence ready for every audit, any day

What drives us

01

Mission

Give every team a simple, strong way to verify identities and control access without slowing anyone down.

How we work

Small teams, close to customers, focused on making access simple and safe.

Principles we never compromise on

01

Verify, then trust

Trust is earned on every request, never assumed from a network.

02

Least privilege

Only the access a task needs.

03

Open standards

SAML, OIDC, SCIM, FIDO2.

04

Privacy by design

We collect only what identity decisions require, and nothing more.

05

Defense in depth

Layered controls mean one failure never exposes everything.

06

Transparent audits

Every decision is logged, searchable and explainable to anyone who asks.
